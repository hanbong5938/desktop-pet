These are acceptance requirements, not claims that the provisional source has passed them.

Configured integrations must actually reconnect. Hiding a pending warning, marking an operation complete, deleting retained journals or manufacturing visible-consent/reconnect acknowledgements is not success. Genuine failures remain observable with their original evidence; uncertain owning-manager outcomes must not be automatically replayed, and user Stop retains its meaning.

The reported permanent reconnect wait has a concrete released-code deadlock: in immutable Core4.8 source2936, normal `retire()` selects `AwaitingReconnect` and releases the migration reservation, while `confirm_tern_reconnect()` subsequently requires `lock_migration_operation()`, which rejects the now-absent owned profile scope. An external acknowledgement therefore cannot complete that normal path. Fresh successor execution must admit authenticated read-only route proof after native Commit but **before retirement**, retaining the bounded owner/profile and current Stop fences. Delivery/reload acknowledgements alone do not authorize retirement.

Historical committed operations require a separate repair path, for both the same-image owner and an authenticated successor owner. The original plan and journal must remain byte-identical; new immutable forward-delivery and settlement receipts must bind the retained original approval/Commit/retirement evidence, exact saved route/profile, current target identity and applicable owner-upgrade proof. Repair must not reacquire fresh migration authority or replay source Stop, target Prepare/Commit, owning managers or retirement. It must wait for legitimate same-profile runtime evidence when the user has closed or stopped the target.

Original profiles, imported characters and their image/profile bindings, dialogue and history must remain intact. Every affected profile needs its own target-start/readiness/Commit and required consumer-binding evidence; one selected profile cannot stand in for the others. The shared approved owner may be retired only after all required profiles/integrations pass. If integration delivery, load or binding fails after native Commit, expose **app committed; integration unavailable; retirement pending**, preserve the old owner/aliases and evidence, and do not imply that retaining a stopped old executable restores connectivity. Unknown manager/delivery outcomes must not be replayed.

The required fresh lifecycle enters explicit `Integrating` only after every affected profile has its own NativeReady and durable Commit evidence. The owner reservation must remain held through integration/retirement resolution while allowing authenticated SDK reuse of an already-committed live instance, not new process spawning. Partial or uncertain Commit must never roll back a committed profile; user Stop must not cause automatic resurrection. Per-profile evidence and secondary-profile references to the central immutable plan remain independent acceptance requirements, not inferred success from a primary profile.
